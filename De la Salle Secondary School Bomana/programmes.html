What students gain

Core knowledge

Students strengthen their learning in language, mathematics and the sciences, laying the groundwork for higher study and practical life skills.

Practical skills

Subjects such as Commerce, Home Economics, Business Studies and Physical Education help students apply learning in everyday life and future work.

Personal growth

Personal Development and Religion support student discipline, behaviour and positive decision-making.

Creative expression

Arts and practical subjects give students space to explore creativity and expression alongside academic learning.
