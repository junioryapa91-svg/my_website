Our story · Our values

A tradition of learning, faith and service.

Discover the people, principles and community that shape life at De La Salle Secondary School Bomana.

De La Salle Secondary School Bomana is a respected Catholic school in the National Capital District. It serves young boys in Bomana, Port Moresby, and provides a learning environment shaped by faith, discipline and academic ambition.

The school is managed by the Catholic Church and is known for helping students develop leadership, responsibility and personal confidence. It is a day school, which means students attend from the local community and families remain closely connected to school life.

Located in Bomana, Port Moresby, Papua New Guinea, the school is easy to reach for families within the National Capital District. With a reported capacity of around 800 students, it provides a welcoming but structured setting for teaching and learning. The atmosphere is designed to help boys become focused, mature and respectful citizens.

Values that shape every day

Faith-based culture

Religion and moral education are important parts of school life, helping learners understand values such as fairness, honesty and kindness.

Balanced learning

The curriculum is designed to connect academic knowledge with practical skills, personal development and social responsibility.

Through daily routines and school activities, students are encouraged to take initiative, show discipline and contribute positively to group life.

School life and values

Students at De La Salle Secondary School Bomana are encouraged to do their best in class while developing practical life skills. The school values effort, responsibility and respect in all parts of daily life.

Learning extends beyond the classroom. Students participate in school routines and activities that develop confidence, discipline and a sense of belonging. This helps them prepare for further study, jobs and active service in their communities.
