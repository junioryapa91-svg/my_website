School profile

Located in Bomana, Port Moresby, the school serves students in the National Capital District and is managed by the Catholic Church.

Daily learning

Students are supported through academic classes, personal development, and school activities that build practical skills and confidence.

School community

As a day school, the college encourages partnership between teachers, students and families in creating a positive learning environment.

A place for growth and achievement

At De La Salle Secondary School Bomana, the school day is designed to help students become disciplined, respectful and eager learners. The environment supports both classroom study and student development beyond the classroom.

Students are encouraged to work hard, cooperate with others and take part in activities that strengthen leadership, teamwork and personal responsibility.

  • Strong focus on literacy, numeracy and scientific understanding
  • Values-based learning grounded in Catholic education
  • Supportive atmosphere for both academic and personal growth

School at a glance
